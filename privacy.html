Privacy.

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

In short

This site is a personal portfolio. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it does not ask you for any personal data.

What loads when you open a page

  • Pages, images, videos and fonts are served from this site's own files. Fonts are self-hosted, so no request goes to Google Fonts or similar services.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • The site remembers your language choice (English or Italian) in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

3D model viewer (Sketchfab)

The 3D Models section shows preview images that are stored on this site. If you click "View in 3D", the viewer is loaded from Sketchfab, a third-party service, and Sketchfab may receive your IP address and set its own cookies. Nothing is loaded from Sketchfab unless you open a model. See the Sketchfab Privacy Policy.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Sketchfab and YouTube lead to services with their own privacy policies. Nothing is loaded from them until you follow a link.

Contact

If you write to battistonmatteo.work@gmail.com, your email address and message are used only to reply to you. For any question about this page, or to ask to have your messages deleted, use the same address.
